Project Overview
========================================================================================================================================================
Personal Finance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a clear overview of personal financial performance. It helps users analyze income, expenses, savings, budget utilization, monthly spending trends, spending categories, and account-wise spending.

**Data Period:** 2018 - 2019(21 Months)

Objectives 
========================================================================================================================================================
* Track total income and actual spending.
* Monitor saving and savings rate.
* Compare budget Vs. actual spending.
* Identify high-spending category.
* Analyze monthly spending patterns.
* Understand spending across different accounts.
* Identify areas where financial planning can be improved.

Dashboard Features
========================================================================================================================================================
*1. Financial KPIs*
 * Total Income
 * Actual Spending 
 * Total Budget
 * Remaining Budget
 * Savings
 * Saving Rate

*2. Budget Vs Actual Analysis*                                                                                                                                      
 Compares actual spending with the total budget across:
  * Mortgage & Rent
  * Home Improvement
  * Groceries
  * Utilities
  * Restaurants

*3. Monthly Spending Analysis*                                                                                                                                             
 * Shows spending trends from **January to December**, helping identify monthly with higher and lower spending.

*4. Spending by Category*                                                                                                                                              
 * The dashboard identifies Mortgage & Rent and Home Improvement as the largest spending categories,
   followed by Groceries, Utilities, and Restaurants.

 *5. Spending by Account*                                                                                                                                                
Spending is also analyzed by account:
 * Checking: $49k
 * Platinum Card: $9k
 * Sliver Card: $5k
